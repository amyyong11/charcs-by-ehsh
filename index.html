Charcs by Ehsh started during COVID as a creative way to bring people together through food. What began as making boards for family and friends has grown into custom charcuterie for all kinds of occasions.

We specialize in curated meats, cheeses, crackers, fruits, and spreads — but also love creating sweet boards, themed boards, and custom options for every event.
